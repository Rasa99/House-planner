What you can do

LandType width x depth in 5 cm steps, or switch on Edit corners and drag, add or remove corners for a land that is not a rectangle.
StreetsClick each side of the land: street or neighbour. Streets are drawn outside with their width. No door or window ever goes on a neighbour wall.
WallsOutside and inside wall thickness: 0.10 / 0.15 / 0.20 / 0.25 / 0.30 m.
Add roomsDrag Bedroom, Living room, Reception, Kitchen, Bathroom, Toilet (WC), Open area or Hall onto the land (or click the tile). Each arrives with furniture, a door and a window. Too big for the gap? It shrinks to fit.
ResizeDrag any wall in 5 cm steps; the room on the other side follows, sizes shown live. Alt + drag moves only one room. Exact sizes can be typed in the inspector.
Move / swapGrab a room by its name to move it; it snaps to the other rooms and stays on the land. Drop it on another room to swap them. Arrow keys nudge 5 cm (Shift 50 cm).
Doors0.80 m and 1.00 m. Drag along a wall or onto another wall. Double-click flips the swing; inspector sets swing in/out and hinge left/right.
Windows0.60, 1.00, 1.20, 1.50, 2.00, 2.50 m. Only on walls facing an open area, the yard or the street. Sizes the room type does not allow are fitted automatically. A window covered by a new room moves itself to a free outside wall.
FurnitureChosen automatically for the room's size and doors, or pick a layout per room (see the next pages). Rearranges itself when the room or its doors change.
Section markDrag in, move the line or its ends, click a head to flip the view, turn 90 degrees, change its letter.
Rules (live)An open area of at least 1.30 x 2.00 m; a bathroom, a toilet, a living room; every room has a window onto open air; window widths suit the room; bedrooms 3.00-5.00 m each way; every room reachable through doors. Click a problem to zoom to it.
FilesSave / Open a .houseplan.json file (moves between computers); autosave; Export DXF in m, cm or mm with real hatches, real dimensions, furniture blocks and named layers. Undo / Redo for everything.
BoardScroll to zoom, drag empty space to pan, F to fit, right-click menu, Delete removes, Esc cancels.
